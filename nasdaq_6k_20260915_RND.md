### Introduction
On September 15, 2026, Instinct Bio Technical Company Holdings Inc. (Nasdaq Symbols: BIOT, BIOTW) announced that on September 9, 2026 and September 10, 2026 it received notifications from the Listing Qualifications Department of The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC ("Nasdaq") about the minimum bid price requirement and the minimum market value of listed securities requirement.

### (A) Sept 9, 2026 notice: Market Value of Listed Securities (MVLS)
- On September 9, 2026, Nasdaq notified the Company that its market value of listed securities ("MVLS") was below US$50,000,000 from July 28, 2026 through September 8, 2026. The Company therefore no longer complies with the continued listing requirement for The Nasdaq Global Market under **Nasdaq Listing Rule 5450(b)(2)(A)**.
- The notice has no immediate effect on the listing or trading of the Company's securities.
- **Compliance period:** 180 calendar days, until **March 8, 2027**.
- **To regain compliance:** MVLS of US$50,000,000 or more for at least **10 consecutive business days** at any time during the compliance period.

### (B) Sept 10, 2026 notice: Market Value of Publicly Held Shares (MVPHS)
- On September 10, 2026, Nasdaq notified the Company that the market value of its publicly held shares was below US$15,000,000 from July 29, 2026 through September 9, 2026. The Company therefore no longer complies with the continued listing requirement for The Nasdaq Global Market under **Nasdaq Listing Rules 5450(b)(2)(C) or 5450(b)(3)(C)**.
- **Compliance period:** 180 calendar days, until **March 9, 2027**.
- **To regain compliance:** MVPHS of US$15,000,000 or more for at least **10 consecutive business days** at any time during the compliance period.
- **Alternative:** the Company may consider applying to **transfer its securities to The Nasdaq Capital Market**, if it meets that market's continued listing requirements.

### (C) Sept 10, 2026 notice: Minimum Bid Price
- On September 10, 2026, Nasdaq notified the Company that the bid price of its ordinary shares was below US$1.00 per share from July 29, 2026 through September 9, 2026. The Company therefore no longer complies with the continued listing requirement for The Nasdaq Global Market under **Nasdaq Listing Rule 5450(a)(1)**.
