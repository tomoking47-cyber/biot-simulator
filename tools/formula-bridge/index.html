Request from Japan

A. Product overview

Please write everything in English. Changes are saved automatically.

B. Base formula (% w/w)

Enter the Indonesian label name (Nama bahan) and %. The Japanese label name (日本語表示名称) is filled in by the button below.

C. Raw material highlights

Features of key raw materials and the supplier's data (efficacy, mechanism, recommended dosage). Attach graphs and documents in section E.

D. Third-party test data

Tests performed by independent laboratories (e.g. patch test, SPF, efficacy, stability, microbiology). Attach the report in section E.

E. Attachments

Data sheets, graphs, specifications, sales materials, third-party reports, SDS, COA. PDF, images, videos and text files up to 20 MB each.

Submit to Japan

When the sample is ready and sections A–E are complete, press Submit. Japan will be notified on this page.
